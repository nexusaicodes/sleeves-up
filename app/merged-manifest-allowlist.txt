# Everything the merged release manifest declares, checked by :app:verifyMergedManifest in both
# directions. One "<kind> <android:name>" per line, and for a component, what decides whether another
# app can reach it: "exported=" (true, false or unset), "permission=" when one guards it, and a
# provider's "authorities=" and "grantUriPermissions=". Each intent filter is a "filter" line naming
# its component, then its actions, categories and data. A <uses-feature> carries "required=", which
# decides the devices Play offers the app to. Meta-data under any element, <property> and <queries>
# entries are listed too. '#' starts a comment. An entry
# is added only after reading what it does: anything that reaches another app, a provider or the
# network, or runs unasked, is removed in AndroidManifest.xml with tools:node="remove" instead. The merger report
# (build/outputs/logs/manifest-merger-release-report.txt) names the library behind each line.
